Green Jena

Green Jena is a multi-user VR experience about summer heat in Jena. Players meet in a virtual model of the city centre, talk about where new trees should go, plant them together from one shared budget and then watch them grow and cool the city over 20 years. The city only cools down fully when all five sites are planted, so players have to weigh the budget against coverage.

Walkthrough and how it's builtFive stages, features, city data and design decisions

One session, five stages

  1. 011:1000

    Intro

    "Jena needs more shade. Five trees, one budget." A tabletop model of the centre rises on a plinth; Start Planting dives into it.

  2. 021:400

    Map

    The model grows into a map in the dark, with "Jena, Thuringia · 38°" and a glowing pin on the JenTower. Point and pull the trigger to enter.

  3. 031:1

    Planting

    Point at a glowing hole to open three species cards. The server checks the shared budget, then spawns a sapling with tree guard, water bag and greening patch.

  4. 040–20 y

    Climate

    Step from today to 20 years ahead in 5-year steps. Trees grow while light and heat change together. Players can return to planting after any step.

  5. 055 / 5

    Results

    After the fifth tree, a board lists the planted species, the remaining budget and the combined scores, then offers to show 20 years of growth.

Diving into the city

Pointing at the JenTower pin zooms the model exponentially around the tower's foot until it reaches the player's feet. It reads as flying in, then the planting scene fades up.

TabletopJenaIntro

Species cards

Turkish Hazel at 4,500 €, London Plane and Honey Locust at 6,000 € each, rated for water, biodiversity, height and shade. Five trees, one 30,000 € budget, so every choice is a trade-off.

CardMenuManager · CardButton

Shared planting

Every player can request a tree; only the server plants it. Trees, remaining budget and climate year live in NetworkVariables, so everyone sees the same city.

PlantingAuthority · TreeGrowth

Wrist timelines

A panel on the left wrist switches between a tree timeline, which grows only the tree you stand at (sapling, young, mature, adding at most 6% cooling), and the climate timeline for the whole city.

TimelineStepper · TimelineManager

Climate and heat look

Today to 20 years in 5-year steps, each played over two seconds. The sun moves from 2000 K to 7800 K while white balance, exposure, bloom and vignette fade the heat haze.

TimelineManager · URP Volume

Locomotion and tree profiles

Thumbstick walking, 2× sprint on the left trigger and jump on A. After planting, a profile panel shows each species growing from a 1.2 m sapling to a 15 m full canopy over 20 years.

A Python converter flattens each LoD2 polygon into its own plane, ear‑clips it into triangles and estimates the ground from the buildings' own ground heights. Coordinates are written as (−East, Height, North) around the Markt, so the map in Unity is north‑up and true to scale. Building data © GDI-Th, CC BY 4.0.

Design decisions

The server decides what is planted

Two players can reach for the same site within one network tick. With a single writer, the budget can't be spent twice and two trees can't share one hole.

Tree size is derived, not networked

Only the year and planted count are synced. Every client computes the same tree sizes and light locally, which keeps growth smooth on a weak connection.

Reversible by construction

Nothing accumulates between frames, so stepping back to today returns exactly the same light and haze, however many times players move along the timeline.
